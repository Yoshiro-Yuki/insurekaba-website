What is the health tracker?

The Health Tracker is a simple guide designed to help you build and maintain a healthier lifestyle. It`s not a strict medical tool, but rather a daily reminder to stay consistent with small habits that improve your well-being—like drinking enough water, getting enough sleep, exercising regularly, and taking care of your mental health. By keeping track of these habits, you can slowly create a routine that supports long-term balance and wellness.
